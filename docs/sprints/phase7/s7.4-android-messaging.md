## 2. Architecture & Reuse
In strict accordance with the Pravah architectural invariants:
- **Core Runtime**: Reused existing `PravahRuntime`, `PravahConfig`, and `LifecycleState`.
- **Physical Transport**: Reused authoritative `TcpTransport` directly (no `AndroidTcpTransport`).
- **Protocol & Framing**: Reused `Message`, `MessageType`, `MessageEncoder`, `MessageParser`, `FrameEncoder`, `FrameDecoder`, `ProtocolSessionManager`, and `PeerConnectionCoordinator`.
- **Application Messaging**: Reused `ApplicationMessage`, `MessageState`, `ConversationManager`, and `DefaultApplicationMessagingService`.
- **Reliability & Delivery**: Reused `DeliveryRetryManager`, `InMemoryDeliveryOutbox`, `OutboxEntry`, and `OutboxState`.
- **Peer Routing**: Reused `PeerRouter(registry, transport)` preserving `PeerId` logical addressing.
- **Persistence**: Used `InMemoryMessageHistoryStore` and `InMemoryDeliveryOutbox` to guarantee clean execution without native SQLite-JDBC binary dependencies on Android.
